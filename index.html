The shift

The AI question has changed.

Capable models now come at very different price points, and open-weight models can run in your own environment. So the useful question is no longer which vendor to buy from. It is how to match each task to the right model, at the right cost, with the right guarantees.

Yesterday
"Which AI vendor should we pick?"

One premium model for everything, per-seat licences, data leaving your environment, and nobody quite sure why the AI answered what it did.

Today
"Which model, for which task, at what cost, and with which guarantees?"

A routing layer sends simple tasks to small, cheap models and complex reasoning to frontier models. Personal data is masked before any prompt leaves your tenant, and every answer is logged with its sources.

Using a frontier model to classify a support ticket is like heating the whole building to warm one room. We make AI economical by design.

Token cost management

You pay for the intelligence a task actually needs.

  • Model routing: small and open-weight models for classification and extraction, frontier models only for complex reasoning.
  • Semantic caching: repeated questions are answered from cache, not re-generated.
  • Metadata first: we send models compact schemas and summaries, not raw data dumps.
  • Budgets and alerts: spending caps per department, use case and user, visible in a dashboard.
🔒

Personal data protection

Sensitive data stays under your control.

  • PII shield: names, emails, IBANs and national IDs are detected and pseudonymised before any prompt is sent.
  • Your tenant, EU region: deployed in your Azure / Fabric environment, with EU-hosted or self-hosted models.
  • No training on your data: enterprise endpoints with contractual zero-retention.
  • Row-level security respected: the AI sees only what the user is allowed to see in Power BI or Qlik.
⎘

Decision traceability

You can answer "why did the AI say that?" for every output.

  • Full audit log: prompt, sources, model and version, cost and output, stored in your lakehouse.
  • Cited sources: every answer links back to the dataset, document or ticket it is based on.
  • Human in the loop: approval steps for actions with business impact.
  • EU AI Act ready: documented use cases, risk classification and monitoring reports.
